Rice Beans Swiss Chard Recipe

Ingredients: (*** = from Food Give Away)

  • 1 cup (cooked) Rice
  • 1 cup (cooked) Beans
  • 1 lb. Hamburger Meat ***
  • 2 Eggs ***
  • 1 Small Onion ***
  • 1 Swiss Chard ***
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes (halved)
  • 1 cup (cooked) Rice ***
  • Olive Oil or Vegetable Oil
  • Italian Seasoning
  • Parmesan Cheese ***

Directions:

  1. Prepare Rice in Rice Cooker. Set aside 1 cup.
  2. Beans from freezer or Cans. Set aside 1 cup.
  3. Cook and Drain 1 lb. of Hamburger Meat with 1 small Onion. Set aside.
  4. Wash and Cut up in small slices 1 Swiss Chard.
  5. 3 Minutes on High. Fry Swiss Chard in Frying Pan with Olive Oil.
  6. 3 Minutes Add 1 Cup Cherry Tomatoes (cut in Half) to Frying Pan
  7. 3 Minutes Add 2 Eggs to Frying Pan and Stir Together.
  8. 3 Minutes Add Meat, Beans, and Rice and Stir
  9. 1 Minute Add in Italian Seasoning and Parmesan Cheese.
  10. DONE!
