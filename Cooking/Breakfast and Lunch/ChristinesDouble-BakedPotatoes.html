Christine’s Double-Baked Potatoes

Ingredients

  • Aluminum Foil
  • Dozen Potatoes
  • Butter
  • Italian Dressing
  • Garlic
  • Shredded Cheese

Directions

  1. Clean Potatoes
  2. Poke holes in Potatoes
  3. Mix Italian Dressing with Garlic
  4. Roll Potato in Dressing Mix
  5. Place in Aluminum Foil
    1. (Single Fold, Long-wise
    2. (Single Fold Width-wise
  6. Bake 400 Degrees for 60 minutes (Small) OR
  7. 450 Degrees 2 HRS (Large)
  8. Remove from oven
  9. Cut Pocket in Middle
  10. Scoop out Potato
  11. Mixer: Mash Potato with Butter and Milk
  12. Fold with Fork Shredded Cheese
  13. Place mix in Potato Pockets
  14. Recover with Foil
  15. Heat until Warm
