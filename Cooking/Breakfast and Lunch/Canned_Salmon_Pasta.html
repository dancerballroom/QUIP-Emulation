Canned Salmon Pasta

Ingredients

  • 2 Cups Pasta (Penne, Spaghetti, Other)
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 2 large cloves garlic minced
  • 1 (5 ounce) can salmon
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice/zest or lime juice
  • 1 tablespoon fresh parsley chopped
  • Salt & pepper to taste
  • Parmesan Cheese

Directions

  • Boil a salted pot of water for your pasta.
  • Cook it al dente according to package directions (9-12min). 
  • Prep your other ingredients while it cooks.
  • Add the oil to a small pan over medium heat. 
  • Once the oil is hot, add the garlic 
  • Cook it for 30 seconds.
  • Stir in the salmon.  Break it up with your fork/flake it, 
  • Add in the juices from the can for more flavor
  • Add the lemon juice + zest, and parsley. Let it heat through. 
  • Once the pasta is done, add a couple teaspoons of the pasta water 
  • Drain the pasta and toss with the sauce. 
  • Season with salt & pepper as needed. 
  • Grate some parmesan on top if you wish.
