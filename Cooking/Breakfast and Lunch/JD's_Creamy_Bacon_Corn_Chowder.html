JD's Creamy Bacon Corn Chowder

Ingredients

  • 1/4 lb. sliced bacon, cut in 1/8ths
  • 1 medium onion, chopped
  • 1/4 cup chopped celery
  • 1/4 cup chopped celery leaves
  • 1/2 cup green peppers
  • 1/2 bay leaf
  • 2 tablespoons flour
  • 1 can whole kernel corn with green peppers and pimiento
  • 3 cups milk
  • 1 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/8 teaspoon pepper
  • 1 1/2 cups shredded Monterey jack cheese

Instructions

  1. Fry bacon.
  2. Remove from pan and drain on paper towels. Set aside for garnish.
  3. Add onion, celery, celery leaves, green pepper, and bay leaf.
  4. Saute about 8 minutes until vegetables are crisp/tender.
  5. Mix in flour, then add undrained corn, milk, salt and pepper.
  6. Cook stirring until soup comes to boil and is slightly thickened.
  7. Just before serving stir in cheese.
  8. Serve garnished with bacon pieces.
