Chicken Alfredo a la Ramen Noodle

REF:  https://www.facebook.com/reel/1666056394323787

Ingredients

  • 6 Chicken Ramen Packages
  • 2 Bottles of Alfredo Sauce
  • 2 Pieces of Chicken, Shredded
  • 1 Cup Water
  • 8 oz. Heavy Cream
  • 1-1/2 Cup Mozzarella Cheese
  • Parmesan Cheese

Directions

  • Place 6 Blocks of uncooked Ramen into the bottom of a greased Casserole dish
  • Spread One Seasoning Packet over all
  • Pour both jars of Alfredo Sauce on top of Ramen
  • Spread out Alfredo Sauce to cover all the Ramen
  • Add Shredded Chicken
  • Add q Cup of Water to First Alfredo Jar, Shake, and Add
  • Add 8 oz. Heavy Whipping cream to Second Alfredo Jar.  Shake and Add.
  • Add Mozzarella Cheese
  • Add Grated Parmesan Cheese
  • Add Italian Seasoning
  • Bake at 350 degrees 45-50 minutes
  • Serve (6) blocks of Ramen when done
