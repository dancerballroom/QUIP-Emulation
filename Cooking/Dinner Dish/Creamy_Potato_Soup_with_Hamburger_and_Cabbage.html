Creamy Potato Soup with Hamburger and Cabbage 

Ingredients

  • 5 lbs. Potatoes, peeled and cored with Apple Slicer
  • 1 lb Hamburger
  • 1 lb Cabbage, chopped, fresh or from fridge/freezer
  • 10 Eggs, ( 5 Boiled, 5 Not Boiled )
  • 1 Small Onion
  • 1 Box Chicken Broth

  • 1 Cup Heavy Whipping Cream
  • 2 Tablespoons Taco Seasoning
  • 2 Tablespoons Seasoned Salt
  • Parsely

Prep Cook

  • Chop up Cabbage
  • Boil 5 Eggs in Egg Cooker
  • Core and Slice 5 lb. Potatoes

Directions

  • Boil Potatoes 15 Minutes in Large Pot 
  • Cook Hamburger, in Frying Pan, Drain
  • Add to Hamburger Onion and Taco Seasoning
  • Boil Cabbage with Potatoes 5 minutes
  • Add 5 Uncooked Eggs
  • Drain Almost all water from Large Pot
  • Add Chicken Broth to Large Pot
  • Add 5 Diced Hard Boiled Eggs to Large Pot
  • Add Hamburgerf
  • Add Heavy Whipping Cream
  • Add Seasoned Salt and Parsley to taste.
  • Cool and Store.
