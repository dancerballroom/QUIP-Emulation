Italian Sausage and Eggplant Casserole

Ingredients

  • 1 whole Large Eggplant
  • ½ teaspoons Salt
  • ½ cups Olive Oil, For Frying
  • 1 cup Milk
  • 1 cup Flour
  • 1-½ pound Ground Italian Sausage
  • 5 cups gravy
  • 4 cups Mozzarella Cheese, Grated

Preparation - Eggplant

  • Peel the skin off the eggplant 
  • Slice in rounds about 1/4 inch thick. 
  • Place slices on a kitchen towel in a single layer. 
  • Moderately salt each slice. 
  • Flip and repeat salting in about 10 minutes.
  • Heat up the olive oil in a skillet medium heat. 
  • Dip each slice in milk and flour.
  • Fry until slightly brown. 
  • Drain oil on a paper towel. 

Preparation - Sausage

  • Make Gravy or Store Bought
    • Melted Butter, 
    • Add Flour opt. Carrots, opt. Celery
    • Add Milk when Browned 
  • Brown and drain the sausage. 
  • Add homemade gravy to the sausage.

Preparation - Casserole

  • Spray casserole dish 
  • 2 tablespoons of gravy on the bottom
  • Place a layer of fried eggplant on the sauce. 
  • Spread part of the sausage on top 
  • Sprinkle the cheese on top of the sausage. 
  • Repeat until all ingredients are used
  • Cheese on Top.
  • Bake covered at 350 degrees for about 45 minutes
