Eggplant Parmigiana

Ingredients (SAUCE + EGGPLANT)

  • 1/4 cup extra-virgin olive oil
  • 3 med. yellow onions, peeled, halved, thin slices
  • 6 cloves garlic, peeled and grated
  • Kosher salt
  • 2 med. eggplants, 1/2-inch thick rounds (2 1/2 LBS)
  • 1/2 cup all-purpose flour
  • Freshly ground black pepper
  • 5 large eggs
  • 3 tablespoons whole milk
  • 4 cups Italian-style breadcrumbs

​

  • 1 tablespoon dried oregano
  • 1 tablespoon crushed red pepper flakes
  • 1 tablespoon granulated sugar
  • 3 (28-ounce) cans whole plum tomatoes
  • 1 tablespoon fresh thyme leaves
  • Vegetable oil, about 1 1/2 to 2 cups
  • 1 1/2 pounds mozzarella cheese, thin slices
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan
  • 1 pound provolone cheese, grated
  • 2 handfuls fresh basil, leaves only, torn

​

dIRECTIONS - For the tomato sauce: 

  • Large skillet, olive oil over med heat. 
  • Add onion and garlic, season with salt. 
  • Cook onions til translucent, 3 to 5 minutes. 
  • Add the sugar and the canned tomatoes.
  • Cook 10 to 15 minutes over medium heat. 
  • Set aside to cool.

DIRECTIONS - For the eggplant

  • Eggplant rounds, 1 layer, 2 baking sheets. 
  • Sprinkle with salt on both sides 1 hour. 
  • Rinse with cold water and dry.
  • Flour in a medium bowl with salt and pepper. 
  • 2nd bowl, whisk the eggs and milk, salt+pepper. 
  • 3rd bowl, combine breadcrumbs, oregano, thyme. 
  • Dip each eggplant slice in flour, shake off excess. 
  • Dip in the egg mixture in the breadcrumbs. 
  • Coat both sides of eggplant slice. 
  • Single layers on the baking sheets.
  • Pour 1/2-inch oil bottom Large Skillet. 
  • Heat the oil until it begins to smoke lightly
  • Kitchen tongs, single layer eggplant to the pan. 
  • Cook 2 min. each side until golden brown. 
  • Remove from the oil, Transfer to a baking sheet. 
  • Preheat oven to 350 degrees F.
  • Bake 350 degrees for 

To assemble:

  • In a 9 by 13-inch baking dish, 1/4 tomato sauce. 
  • Top with a layer of the fried eggplant
  • Top with about 1/3 of the mozzarella slices. 
  • Sprinkle 1/4 Parmesan and provolone cheeses. 
  • Top with a layer of torn basil leaves.
  • Spoon sauce repeat layering 2 times = 3 layers. 
  • End with the remaining mozzarella.
  • Carefully press the layers down firmly . 
  • Cook until the cheese is melted 35 to 40 min. 
  • Extra browning, dish under the broiler @1 minute.
