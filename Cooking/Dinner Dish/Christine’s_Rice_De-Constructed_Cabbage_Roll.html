Christine’s Rice De-Constructed Cabbage Roll

Ingredients

  • 1 Head of Cabbage
  • 15 oz. Can Diced Tomatoes
  • 15 oz. Can of Peas
    15 oz. Cat of Carrots
  • 1 lb. Sausage
  • 1 lb. Hamburger
  • 2 cups rice (any type) (broken is awesome)
  • 2 Tablespoons Beef bouillon
  • 1 oz. Ginger Marinade

Directions

  • Rice Cooker to cook rice with beef bouillon and 2 tablespoons oil
  • Chop Cabbage into 1 inch pieces (Save some leaves to cover the top) and set aside
  • Brown the Sausage and set aside
  • Brown the Hamburger and set aside
  • Spray Casserole Dish with Non-Stick Cooking Spray
  • Preheat oven at 350 degrees.
  • In Big Bowl mix together:
    • Rice
    • Meat
    • Shredded Cabbage
    • Pour Undrained tomatoes over mixture.
    • Can of Peas
    • Can of Carrots
    • Marinade
  • Pour in Casserole
  • Add Cabbage leaves over top
  • In Oven 20 minutes 

Ginger Marinade Ingredients

  • Ginger: Fresh grated or ground ginger provides warmth and spice.
  • Soy Sauce: Adds saltiness, umami, and color (or use tamari/coconut aminos).
  • Garlic: Fresh minced or powdered garlic for pungent flavor.
  • Oil: Sesame oil for nutty aroma, or a neutral oil.
  • Acid: Rice vinegar or lime juice brightens flavors and helps tenderize.
  • Sweetener: Honey, brown sugar, or mirin balances the saltiness.
  • Optional Add-ins: Sesame seeds, black pepper, red pepper flakes, green onions, or even pineapple juice.
