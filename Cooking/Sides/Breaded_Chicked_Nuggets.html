Breaded Chicken Nuggets
Breading Steps
  • Set up stations: Put a binder like 1 beaten egg or 3 tablespoons of melted butter in one bowl, and 1 cup of bread crumbs (or Panko) mixed with salt and garlic powder in a second bowl.
  • Dip the chicken: Take a warm or thawed cooked nugget and dip it completely into the egg or melted butter.
  • Coat in crumbs: Transfer the nugget to the bread crumbs and press gently so the crumbs stick to all sides.
Reheating and Crisping
  • Prep the tray: Place the breaded nuggets in a single layer on a greased baking sheet or wire rack.
  • Add oil: Lightly spray or drizzle the top of the crumbs with oil or cooking spray so they turn golden and crunchy.
  • Bake: Heat in an oven at 400°F (200°C) for 8 to 10 minutes, just until the bread crumbs turn brown and crisp, since the chicken inside is already fully cooked.
