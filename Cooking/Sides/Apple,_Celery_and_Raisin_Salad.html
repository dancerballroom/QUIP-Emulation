Apple, Celery and Raisin Salad

Ingredients:

  • 2 cups apples, chopped
  • 1 1/2 cups celery, chopped
  • 1/2 cup raisins
  • 1/2 cup sunflower seeds
  • 1/4 cup mayonnaise

Directions:

  • Combine all ingredients together. 
  • Add more mayonnaise if needed. 
  • Serve chilled.
