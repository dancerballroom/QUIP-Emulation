Christine’s Yams / Sweet Potatoes

Ingredients

  • 3 Large Sweet Potatoes
  • Olive Oil
  • Salt
  • Foil
  • Miniature Marshmallows

Directions

  • Pre-Heat Oven 400 degrees
  • Wash and Cut off Bad Parts of the Sweet Potatoes
  • Douse with Olive oil
  • Sprinkle on Salt
  • Wrap Sweet Potatoes in Aluminum Foil
  • Place on Baking Sheet for 90 minutes
  • Mash Sweet Potatoes with a handful of Miniature Marshmallows.
