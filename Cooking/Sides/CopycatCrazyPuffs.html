Copycat Crazy Puffs

Ingredients

    • 2 (13.8-ounce) pizza crust (such as Pillsbury)
    • ⅔ cup unsalted butter, melted
    • 2 tablespoons finely chopped fresh parsley
    • 4 teaspoons dried Italian seasoning
    • 4 teaspoons garlic salt
    • ½ teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
    • 3 cups jarred pizza sauce
    • 3 cups pepperoni slices
    • 8 cups pre-shredded farmer-style mozzarella cheese
    • ½ cup freshly grated Parmesan cheese

    Instructions 

Directions

    1. Preheat the oven to 375 degrees F (180 degrees C) 
    2. Place a baking sheet on the bottom rack oven. 
    3. Coat a shallow 12 cup muffin tin with cooking spray.
    4. Roll pizza dough across prepared muffin tin (Cover the entire surface) 
    5. Using a pizza cutter, cut dough into squares for each muffin tin. 
    6. Discard any excess dough.
    7. Press and roll dough squares into each tin (at least halfway up the sides of each cup)
    8. Stir together butter, parsley, Italian seasoning, garlic salt, and black pepper in a small bowl.
    9. Brush butter mixture onto each dough cup.
    10. Dollop 1 teaspoon pizza sauce into each cup. 
    11. Layer two slices of pepperoni, add 1 tablespoon cheese overtop.
    12. Add another 2 slices of pepperoni. 
    13. Top with another 2 tablespoons of cheese.  
    14. Press the cheese within each muffin cup. 
    15. Add Parmesan to butter mixture, and drip butter mix over each puff.
    16. Bake in the preheated oven until cheese is melted, about 15 minutes. 
    17. Set oven to broil, and broil until golden brown, about 1 minute. 
    18. Remove from oven and allow to cool for 2 minutes before serving.
