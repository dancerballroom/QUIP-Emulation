Air Fryer and Apple Peeler Potato Chips

Ingredients

  • 2 large potatoes
  • Olive oil-flavored cooking spray
  • 1/2 teaspoon sea salt
  • Minced fresh parsley, optional

Directions

  • Preheat air fryer to 360°. 
  • Using an Apple Peeler, cut potatoes into very thin slices. 
  • Transfer to a large bowl; add water to cover. 
  • Soak for 15 minutes; drain. 
  • Add more water and soak another 15 minutes.
  • Drain potatoes; place on towels and pat dry. 
  • Spritz potatoes with cooking spray; 
  • Sprinkle with salt. In batches, 
  • Place potato slices in a single layer on greased tray in air-fryer basket. 
  • Cook until crisp and golden brown, 
  • 15-17 minutes, stirring and turning every 5-7 minutes. 
  • If desired, sprinkle with parsley.
