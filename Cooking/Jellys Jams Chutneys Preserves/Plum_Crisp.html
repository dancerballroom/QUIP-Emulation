Plum Crisp

(2x Recipe from AllRecipes.com)

Ingredients
24 ripe plums, pitted and chopped
2 cups white sugar, divided
2 cups sifted all-purpose flour
1 tablespoon baking powder
½ teaspoon salt, or to taste
2 large beaten egg
1 cup melted unsalted butter
Directions
Step 1:  Preheat the oven to 350 degrees F (175 degrees C). 
            Grease an 8x8-inch baking dish.
Step 2:  Spread chopped plums evenly into the prepared baking dish 
            and sprinkle with 1/4 cup sugar.
Step 3:  Mix flour, remaining 3/4 cup sugar, baking powder, and salt together 
            in a large bowl; stir in beaten egg. 
            Spoon mixture over plums and drizzle batter with melted butter.
Step 4:  Bake in the preheated oven until fruit is bubbling and topping 
            is golden brown and crisp, about 40 minutes.
