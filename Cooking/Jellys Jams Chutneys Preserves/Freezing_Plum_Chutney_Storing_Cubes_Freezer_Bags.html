Can I store Plum Chutney in a freezer bag in the freezer?

Freezing Steps
  • Cool the plum chutney completely.
  • Spoon the chutney into ice cube trays.
  • Freeze until solid.
  • Transfer the frozen cubes into a freezer-safe bag.
  • Press out extra air, seal, and label the bag.
Best Practices
  • Portion control: Freezing in cubes lets you thaw only what you need.
  • Thawing: Move portions to the refrigerator to thaw before serving.
  • Quality: Use the frozen chutney within 2 to 3 months for the best flavor and texture
