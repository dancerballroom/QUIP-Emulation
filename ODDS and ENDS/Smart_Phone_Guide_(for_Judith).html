Smart Phone Guide (for Judith)

  • Types of Input (with stylus or fingers)  NOTE:  If you don’t know what to do try various Input types.
    • Click to OPEN app ICON. (Phone, Photo, Gallery, Facebook, Quip,  Messaging, Messenger)
    • Click to CLOSE app or Hang up (Pressing the “X” or pressing the Red Hang-up button)
    • Press and Slide Up (To answer phone, To unlock phone)
    • Press and Slide Down from TOP (To open Notifications Page, to Open Settings Page)
    • Press and Slide Up from Bottom (To open hidden ICONS or other controls or APPS)
    • LONG-Press (“1” for Voicemail in Phone App, “2” for Regina, “3” for Larry, etc.), Move Screen Icon from Home Screen or from App list onto Home Screen
    • Slide Left to Right (Access PREVIOUS screen)
    • Slight Right to Left (Access NEXT screen)
    • Click both fingers together and separate slowly (Zoom In Picture)
    • Click both fingers separately and move together (Zoom In Picture)
