MORNING: The 8am Opener’s Checklist

  • ARRIVAL
    • Check Judith’s Phone for Charge.  Put it on the Charger.(White cable next to her lift chair)
    • Provide Judith her left ear hearing aid (On the dining/living room Table)
    • Mark readings on Calendar (next to stair well)
  • Put away other hearing aid in charger (It’s next to bed on bedside table, also it’s cable is there)
  • PRE-BREAKFAST
    • Place AM Meds for current day in pill box in wooden bowl on TV tray.
    • Oximeter upon Judith’s request.  Blood Pressure upon Judith’s request.
    • Place short glass of Orange Juice and a shot of Cranberry Juice on TV tray.
    • Put apron on Judith and place tv tray in front of her to take pills.
    • Heat up coffee in tall cup (next to sink) (usually 30 seconds to 1 minute in the microwave)
  • BREAKFAST
    • Oatmeal: 1 packet (in above cupboard left of fridge), milk in fridge, 1/2 banana sliced on top.
    • After taking away dishes, provide Judith
      • Toothbrush and Toothpaste
      • 1 glass of water and a square Tupperware (Spittoon)
    • Remove afterward and rinse in sink.  Place in dish washer
    • Start dish washer if full.  Cascade is under the sink. 
  • DAILY HOUSE WORK
    • Check Toilet (Check throughout the day)
      • Seat askew?
      • Toilet Paper out? (Under counter)
      • Discrete Underwear out? (Next to bed in bedroom, behind mirror)
      • Dirty underwear and wipes in kitchen trash
    • Put Judith’s tablet on the charger (On the bed, may be covered)
    • Fix Bed, Turn off light, Check temperature on Thermostat.  (Next to bedroom door on wall)
    • Feed/Water inside and outside cats (Food in Garbage Can near back door)
    • Check Food for Finches (on the porch on the corner in a Pitcher)
    • Check Food for Hummingbirds: 1-1/3 cup Sugar, fill up with water.
  • LEAVING in the MORNING checklist
    • Ask if Judith has access to her Life Alert Pendant
    • Check her cell phone is charged 90 to 100 percent. (White cable next to her lift chair)  
    • Charging cables are available next to her TV chair and her bedroom night table.
    • Give Judith her tablet
    • Check bathroom for enough pads, toilet paper.
    • Make sure her way to the bathroom is clear.  Make sure her TV tray is removed.
  • Paths to bathroom and bedroom are clear. (Check throughout day)
    • Check Judith’s Phone for Charge.  Put it on the Charger.(White cable next to her lift chair)
