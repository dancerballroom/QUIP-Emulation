Taking Care of the pool

4 Pool Tests for balancing the chemicals with Chlorine, Soda Ash, and Acid:  1.  Chlorine/Bromide2.  pH Test3.  Acid Demand/Alkalinity Test4.  Total Alkalinity Test

Given:
5 Chemical Test Bottles    #1 - Yellow Cap    #2 - Red Cap    #3 - White Cap     #4 - Green Cap     #5 - Blue Cap

1 Test Kit with 2 tubes (1 large, 1 small)

Tests
1.  Chlorine/Bromine Test     Fill small tube with pool water 18'' under surface          and fill tube to the "cl" marking     Place 1 "cl" tablet into small tube and shake     OR Mix 5 drops #1 Yellow cap     Ideal reading for Chlorine:  2.5 to 3.5           above 3.5 too much chlorine:  no chlorine           below 1.0 too little chlorine:  add chlorine2.  pH Test     Fill large tube with pool water 18'' under surface          and fill tube to the "pH" marking      Mix 1 drop #4 Green cap       Mix 5 drops #2 Red cap      Ideal reading for pH:  7.4 to 7.6           7.2 indicates too much acid:  add soda ash           7.8 indicates to little acid:  add acid       Read "Base Demand/pH Increaser Chart" to determine       amount of soda ash or Baking Soda to add       per capacity of pool in Gallons (12,757.5)3.  Acid Demand and Alkalinity Test     Using pH Test Water from pH Test     Mix 1 drop #3 White cap and swish, count drops     Continue Mixing/Counting 1 drop #3 until matches 7.4 color     (Do not count drops with 7.2 reading or lower)     Read "Acid Demand Chart" in booklet to determine      acid per capacity of pool in Liters.     (12,757.5 gallons = 48292.4 liters)4.  Total Alkalinity Test     Using large pH tube     Fill large tube with pool water 18'' under surface          and fill tube to the "Total Alkalinity" marking      Mix 1 drop #4 Green cap and Swirl      Mix 2 drops #5 Red cap and Swirl      Count drops of #3 White Cap after add and Swish               turning water clear or light yellow.       Multiply (number of drops) x 10 = part per million(ppm)       Ideal Alkalinity:  80 to 100       If (number of drops) < 8 (80ppm) add sodium bicarbonate       If (number of drops) > 10 (10ppm) add Acid (do Acid Demand test).
