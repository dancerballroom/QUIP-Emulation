Doctor's Appointments / Other Appointments

Schedule Para-Transit

    •  1 week ahead or less (at least 2 days ahead if possible)
    • Judith Calls B-Line Para-Transit
      • 1st Menu:  Press 2
      • 2nd Menu:  Press 0
      • 3rd Menu:  Press 1
    • Up to 15 minute wait on hold usually or hang up and try later

Catch Para-Transit (Prepare)

    • Judith to use bathroom at least 1/2 hour before arrival
    • Have the Mask out of her purse.
    • Pull out two $5 bills (costs $3.50) for trip
      • Assistants ride for free
      • I typically drive the car behind the bus and meet it at the destination
    • Dress Judith before the Bus arrives
      • Socks and Shoes
      • Pajama Top with sleeves
    • Additional Prep:
      • Feet for Wheelchair are on the porch outside the glass screen door.
      • Judith takes her purse, sometimes a tissue
      • You can open the piano room to watch for the bus arrival
      • (Typically you hear a beeping.)
      • Wave to the driver to acknowledge you are getting ready

Para-Transit Embarking and Disembarking

    • Para-Transit can arrive 15 minutes before or 15 minutes after scheduled time
    • Wait in a safe area on the pavement for the bus driver to lower the lift and take over.
    • Driver may need assistance to lock the wheel chair
    • Driver may need assistance in the bus
    • After helping the driver, place the $5 in the pay slot (at the front of the bus)
    • Clarify the destination before letting the driver leave.
    • If there is more than 1 passenger clarify how long it will take Judith to arrive.
