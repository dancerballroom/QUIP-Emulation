-Excerpt from the film "Little Big Man"
as I transcribed it.

An Analysis: What does the Grandfather's action represent?
It's an act of salvation by the Grandfather who recognizes his son loss
of sanity and purpose in life. He uses his own life and ritual of death as
an example by metaphor in order to bring a perspective on life and
hope back into his Grandson's existence. Grandfather's act does not lead to his
death, but does succeed in objectifying the negativity of life's remorse and
failure with the positivity of life's happyness and success.

Spiritually it asks for reflection on the greater truth and
perspective in terms of life as a whole.

Ultimately, who but God can know what is Good or Bad for us in our lifetime.
Our purpose here is to learn and grow from our experience, and if
there be a grander scheme, let God take care of it, and don't worry about
being Good enough or Smart enough or Successful enough. In the end we
all go the same path so we might as well forgive ourselves and move on.
