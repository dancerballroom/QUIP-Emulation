02-03-2005    Self imposed 3rd Person therapy

  • Feb. 3rd, 2005 at 1:06 AM
I am Larry and today Larry is a 3rd Person.

Larry went around town to all the special work search spots yesterday:
RUSH, Anderson & Associates, EDD, and tomorrow he's going to the Career Fair. He doesn't typically find the Career Fairs particularly
beneficial. They do offer him a good opportunity to pick up toys and pens. Of course, over the years the toys have decreased, but it's always worth his visit if by chance he can nab a flashing YoYo or spongy frisbee or cool looking pen. Larry remembers the cool pin light and black pen he got from the CIA one year back in 1999. He also likes to visit info meetings for jobs he won't necessarily get just for the pizza and soda(Too bad they don't serve Pale Ale).

Larry likes to play on the computer. When he gets home these days he likes to fool around on the piano for an hour or so and then get to work (not really - 20% work, 80% play and procrastination). He has a lot of fun with friends just talking techie and pa(w)l(ing) around the coffee shoppes. (Damn expensive habbit if you were to ask him, but priceless to be with friends he knows and loves.)

To be honest, Larry didn't always like coffee, you know. It's more a social drink that has a drug in it (sugar? or caffeine? pick your poison) than something you really feel tastes good.

Larry is both at ease with himself and at odds with his world. By his opinion he would have probably been better off having been born either in the 1500's as a farmer or in the future as a Transformer (He kind of likes the evil transformers). Of course, for you reincarnation gods up there, note: being a member of the Red Team or the Blue Team would also be acceptable.

Oh, and before he goes, Larry should reflect on a Bush Sr. 3rd person
moment when he was on TV advertising for re-election.

(Larry is an excellent choice for President. Vote for Larry.)
Of course, Larry doesn't really want to be President. Now, being a lobbyist for a Credit Card Bank Cartell, that might be more exciting.

Larry just watched Harold and Kumar go to white Castle. Definitely a movie you want to watch while in an influenced state of mind. Kind of reminds me of Cheech and Chong. Larry would like to experience this some day. (Too bad that job hunting includes invasive Lemon Snow Cone Testing)

It's funny how being a third person really frees up your thoughts.

Have a nice day.

- Larry, ( The "HE IS" )
