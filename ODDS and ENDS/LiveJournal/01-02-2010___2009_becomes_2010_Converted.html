What's in the number?

Whether it's an age where it's a year, it's another day realizing that you are you are. Time marches on our bodies change we reached landmarks in our lives that show us time is precious.

I remember that before 1990 I used to measure time as the distance between my birth and the year 2000. in my early teenage years I used to think that the year 2000 was so far away that I was going to be an old person by the time that year arrived.

Each and every person I know as achievements and failures. Regardless of our age thing but each one of us try to reach out and touch other, and leave part of a cells of the mark world.

What do these numbers mean to you?

5, 9, 12, 16, 18, 21, 25, 29, 36, 44, 64, 73, 80.

Five is the year that I can honestly say I had consciousness of self.
Nine was the year I became aware of sexuality.
12 was the last year of my childhood.

At 16 I worked away from home on an Indian reservation for the youth conservation Corps of California.

At 18 I had to make decisions about my future as an adult.
At 21 I took my first alcoholic drink although I did not like alcohol.

25 was the year that I envisioned that I would have a wife a place to live a solid profession and a bright future with a lot of possibilities.

As it so happens 29 is the age that I felt the most like for 15 years.

At my 36th year of life a lot of changes took place that were on expect and I made decisions that may have led paths and choices that I could not foresee the outcome of. Neither good nor bad, it was an age of transition.

Now to switch focus. 64 was that age with my mother when she died of breast cancer. 73 is the average life span for my family in general in the distant past.
80 seems to be here of a new rebirth of people to live that long.

I like to contemplate the years in the days, the hours and minutes and seconds, and how many may have gone before and how many come afterward.

I would like to think that the happy times have been worth it and that there are many more to come.

Years of education have made me aware of my ignorance.
Contemplation and listening to others have made me aware of their defined contribution in God's creation.

As years go by and doors closed behind me I thank God for all the opportunities I've had and all the wonderful people that I've either been acquainted with or currently know. All in all its not a bad deal.
