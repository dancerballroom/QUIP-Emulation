06-24-2023 Two Twenties Nineteen

This being the 23rd year of the 21st Century, to which my additional six and thirty years of 20th Century experience thereto I may add, I have come across the realization that I, though not willingly so, am of the oldest of my family's lineal generation to guide my family into the future.
 
My Step Mother, Judith Davies, having lived some four score and seven years had but passed this last 7th of May unexpectedly of natural causes in her sleep, which is truly a blessing to her, and one would hope that most would experience a similar passing.

After each family member's passing perspective seems ever to change. So peculiar to see myself as both the subject of my own story in the before and the after each event. Reevaluations of Values: What's important, What are the Goals, How to think and reflect, How to move forward.

Soon I will say goodbye to my Family home of 50 years to a Real Estate sale. The place was full of memories, a safe haven, quiet, private, unique. I will miss it for its solitude and at the same time I will be happy to see it sold, with all it's maintenance and worries.

And yet knowing there are more years behind me than before, I realize that embracing change and being thankful for each day is the example I would like to pay forward. The same gift that has been paid forward to me with love, forgiveness, and focus on compassion.

NOTE: I never liked the idea of turning 60, so I cheat. In French, 70 to 80 is 60-ten, 60-eleven, 60-twelve, ... 60-nineteen, just like 90-100, Four-20s-Ten, Four-20s-Eleven, Four-20s-Twelve, ... Four-20s-Nineteen, So a friend of mine suggested that I borrow their own logic and create Two-20s-Nineteen for (59), which is Dang close to Three-20s (which of course isn't valid French, but who cares!!!).
