Are my dogs atheists? Do you think that it matters to God? From what I can hear my dogs might call their God (or Godly prophets) "Ralph" or "Ruf" or "Wolf" or "Woof". God grant that they are praying to the right one. Likewise, God grant I'm praying to the right one. Ironic that "D-O-G" in the English language should be opposite "G-O-D", or that "L-O-V-E" be opposite "E-V-O-L". Guess it's E-V-O-L-ution I s'pose. The literalistic faith-master who reads the "Good News", the "Vulgate", the "Φως του Θεού" tells me that God only speaks English. (Too bad in my education I learned Deutsch, Français, Español, and some 日本語, among others. It makes me a horrible literalist.)

Maybe my dogs like Yoga. They seem to contort their bodies in such a relaxed manner with ease. I have never taken Yoga myself, and Yoga for me at my age combined with my BMI might not be wise, so I leave the Yoga to my dogs. God bless them.

My dogs have each other, and myself. They share with me in joy and fellowship in the church of my couch and a much enjoyed belly rub. They wag their tails in a show of enjoyment and ecstasy. My dogs might have a religion, I just don't know. Could I pass judgment on them if I did know? What if their belief didn't coincide with mine? Would I love them less?

I choose not the road of judgment, fear and arrogance. I just love my dogs for who they are. Seems to me that EVOL starts with the backward idea that unconditional LOVE requires discrimination.

Thus I pray that God inspire me to do that which is right. I pray that through divine insight I cause no pain to my dogs. I would like to share my love with my dogs and dogs belonging to others, as to best provide them with comfort and peace.

Respectfully,

-Larry Davies
