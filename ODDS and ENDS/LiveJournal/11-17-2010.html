1. Wow, that was easy! A doctor visit in Germany.

The German doctor looked at me and said "Don't worry about the insurance."
as he gave me the necessary medication and treatment to overcome my first
instance of Chronic Bronchitis.

There was no receptionist to give me a hard time or make me fill out hours of
paperwork. There was practically no delay in seeing the doctor, and the doctor
gave me an injection and medication to take home at no charge.

There I was, feeling like a dummy, having postponed going to the doctor.
For three weeks of constant coughing I had denied myself treatment, partially
believing I just needed to be a man and overcome, and secondly for
fear of having no health insurance. I simply hoped against hope that the
coughing would cease. (What really got me later was that if I had just gone to
the doctor a little sooner the chronic bronchitis condition would never
have developed. A condition that would recur about once every 3-5 years
thereafter.)

I visited again the following year. The farmer's wife, Karen Kühlman,
had birthed a child whose eyelids were inverted on birth. This, it was
guessed, might have been caused by Chernobyl, which had taken place
during the childs gestation period) Karen spent months in the hospital
with her child. When I inquired about the medical cost the farmer replied
that instead of loosing the farm, 6 months of care had simply cost
about $200.00 for medication.

"Du bist einfach irre nicht zum Artzt zu gehen," I remember hearing Karen
tell me. (You are simply wrong not to go see the doctor). I could only
tell the farmer's wife that I was simply behaving the way an American
without insurance would, even today (Don't get it checked until you're
dying.) Pretty stupid but not uncommon, huh.

I was so embarrassed. Later when I worked for a year in Germany I paid my
$60.00 a month for comprehensive care. I had Scheine (Coupons) that I could
use at any hospital to cover my eye sight and my dental and any other
health issues I might have.

To this day I realize that in 1986 and 1990 and 2010 Germany takes care
of it's citizen's health better than the United States ever will, and
with the political climate the way it is, nothing is due to change in
my life time. Give it another generation, maybe. Too many greedy people
running the show right now. People who believe that we live in the greatest
country on earth.
