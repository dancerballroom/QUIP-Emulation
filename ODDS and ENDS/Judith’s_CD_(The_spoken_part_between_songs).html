Judith’s CD (The spoken part between songs)

  • This musical CD is dedicated to a truly sophisticated and elegant Lady.  She was soft spoken, and very humble about her many skills in Music, Languages, Acting, Reading, Journaling, and caring about all people.  Judith Davies had at an early age reached a concert pianist level when she was a young girl.  Outside in the 70’s listening to her play one could hear her practicing: “Rhapsody in Blue for Piano Solo” As a beginning Piano player I would play pieces that were at most 2 pages.  Her music was 30+ pages.  She made it seem easy.  It was so inspiring..  Here is an excerpt...
  • In the 1980’s Grieg’s Concerto in A minor for two pianos was the most advanced piano piece I remember hearing Judith playing with “Aunt Alma.”, also an accomplished pianist, accompanist, and piano instructor.
  • Judith used Spanish at her place of employment to help people.  She had studied Spanish for a couple years in Mexico City.  One of the songs she asked me to turn into Karaoke for the VFW was Piel Canela.  The lyrics are a poem of demotion to a woman with Cinnamon colored skin.
  • I remember Judith telling me a story about passing out programs at her University for a young Elvis, who was just becoming popular.  Here is a touch of Elvis...
  • Judith had a Classical Radio Program for more than 10 years from the 2000’s to 2019.  She created her own programs and liked to combine the show with program notes about the music, the composers, and the artists.  One of the first CDs I remember she played was “The Planets” by Gustav Holst.  Here is the Bringer of Jollity.
  • In 1981, Judith invited me to join her in a Community Chorus class at Oroville High School. Here are two songs I remember from that period.  Sentimental Journey and Moon River.
  • In the 70’s one could often hear Judith play piano and sing songs out of her 60’s Song book “Both Sides Now” was one of her favorites.  Also “Come Together by The Beatles”, “For Me and My Gal“ by Judy Garland, and Judy Garland’s Daughter ”Liza Minnelli’s Ring them Bells“ and ”Yes“  
  • Around the house we often heard an eclectic selection of Pop and Classic Music.  Judith had seen the musical HAIR when it first came out.    Here are a couple of selections...  Air, Aquarius, and Hair from the musical HAIR.
  • Judith was very passionate about supporting the arts and she was often in Community plays at the Oroville Birdcage theater.  One of her favorite musicals in the last couple of years was Hamilton.  Here is an excerpt from King George III “You’ll be back.” from the Musical Hamilton written by Lin-Manuel Miranda.
  • Judith loved to go to Karaoke at the VFW.  She enjoyed singing and visiting with family and friends and have a glass of white wine.  She sang many songs. I helped create the CD’s for various songs.  Here is Blackbird by the Beatles.
  • Judith also liked to sing as a duo or as part of a group.  Hear is “Red Solo Cup”
  • Opera was a really big event for Judith.  We would watch the Metropolitan Opera at Cinemark 10 theaters in Chico.  Some of the performances were 4, 5, and 6 hours, with intermissions.  One of the many pieces performed included the Barber of Seville and Carmen.  Here are short songs from these two selections.
  • Back to Judith’s KRBS Radio show. She volunteered and had Classical programs that included “Fanfare for the Common Man” by Copland, Falla’s “El Amor Brujo” (The Lovely Witch: The Ritual Fire Dance) Rachmaninov’s “Rhapsody on a theme of Paganini”.
  • I think that Judith would appreciate the final selections here. The Entertainer by Scott Joplin and Spike Jone’s and his City Slickers “Cocktail’s for Two“
  • Judith went to a Catholic School in her youth.  So in Closing:  Judith, May the joy that you shared with everyone you touched in your life find you blessed.  We Love you Judith.  Here’s “You’re never fully dressed without a smile.”  from the musical Annie.
