Memories of my experiences with Judith Davies by Larry Davies, stepson

  • Growing Up, Don’t know how to spell a word or need a definition.  Judith was our go-to person.  She was an avid reader.  She had studied Latin, spoke fluent Spanish, and was a fan of Italian Opera.
  • Judith was really good at keeping calm in most situations, but occasionally kitchen cabinets would be closed with extra emphasis and volume.
  • For at least a year Judith gave me rides up the hill from the Social Security office or the Butte County Library during my Senior year in High School.
  • After my music studies at the University I decided to buy a piano.  I bought it over 5 years with very hefty monthly payments earning minimum wage and squeaking by., To my surprise Judith paid it outright as a Christmas gift.
  • Judith was very active in her AAUW (American Association of University Women).  She organized Play readings, she took the minutes for the KRBS radio station, and she had a weekly Classical program.  She organized and read the history notes for the composers, performers, and performances.  
  • I learned how to cook working for Judith.  Judith was OK to try new things, and most of those worked.  I keep a list of years of recipes available.
  • Tom and Judith employed me at the beginning of 2013 to help up the hill two times a week mainly with the job of bringing the Recology Bins up the Hill.  This responsibility grew rapidly into many other jobs and after my Dad Tom Davies passing in March of 2014, I started full time care for Judith.  This turned out to be 9 years.  Judith was very pleasant to be around and I am happy I was able to fill the role of care giver.
  • Judith was always well dressed, classy, well educated, sharp mentally and modest.  Though sometimes expressing frustration of her declining health she sought harmony and always she was always considerate.  Having life experience from 1935 onward, Judith lived life from a patriotic time in history and she had a positive feeling on the future of America.  She was passionate about people taking care of people.  She believed that the future could and would be better.
  • Mostly I will remember Judith loving Karaoke, Classical Music, watching Turner Classic Movies, going out on the porch and looking over the valley, watching the finches and hummingbirds from her chair and loving on her cats  enjoying some wine with lunch and dinner.
