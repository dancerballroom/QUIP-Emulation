EVENING:  The 7pm Closer’s Checklist

  • Check Cell Phone.  Put on Charger next to Judith if needed.
  • 1 Baclofen Tablet Before Dinner
  • Feed Judith Dinner on TV Table - Leftovers in Tupperware in the fridge
  • After taking away dishes, provide Judith
    • Toothbrush and Toothpaste
    • 1 glass of water and a square Tupperware (Spittoon)
  • Remove afterward and rinse in sink.  Place in dish washer
  • Start dish washer if full.  Cascade is under the sink. 
  • Ask if Judith wants to have her hearing aid charged.
    • The new hearing aid box is on the dining room table.
    • The old hearing aid box is on her night table in her bedroom.
  • Ask if Judith has access to her Life Alert Pendant
  • Judith has her tablet and it is charged.
    • Uses same charging cable as her cell phone.
  • Paths to bathroom and bedroom are clear.
    • Check bathroom for enough pads, toilet paper.
  • Bedroom Touch-Light is on high so she can see when she goes to bed.
  • Bed covers are drawn so Judith can get into bed easily.
  • Place Tissue Box in reach of Judith on her right side when laying down.
  • On night table, reachable items should include
    • Chapstick
    • Lotion 
    • Bed control
    • Touch lamp
  • Ask Judith if she wants her socks on/socks off.
  • Before leaving turn Kitchen light, porch light, stair light off.
    • Leave light on in the bathroom and the front porch light.
  • Make sure doors are shut and windows are shut if cold or when it’s warm if it requested.
  • Pull down sun blocking blanket and pull up sun blocking blinds.
  • Check her cell phone is charged and put on the Charger next to Judith if needed.  
  • Make sure her way to the bathroom is clear.  Make sure her TV tray is removed
  • Do everything I may have missed in this list...
  • Lastly, Go over with Judith the checklist:  Pendant, Charged Phone, Bedroom Light On, etc.
