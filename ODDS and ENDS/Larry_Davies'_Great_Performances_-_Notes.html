Larry Davies' Great Performances - Notes

This is a disk of 22 of the best music I've created.  

Composer's Note:
Please listen to my music on a sound system with left and right speakers.  Many of the pieces I've created will sound significantly better in a Car with stereo or headphones with left and right channels.

1)  Life is a Carousel
At The Gilbert family reunion in Oregon many moons ago, I remember my family riding on a Carousel in Downtown Salem, OR.

2)  Bacchanalia
It's a play on words.  A little Bach-like, a little Bacchus-like, played on a Synthesizer version of a Church Organ

3) Tuesday Night Improv
It was a Tuesday night.  I layered the tracks bottom to top in 2 hours.

4) Take Action Now
Call to keep Politicians more honest and Voters more aware.

5) Halloween Minimal
Slight of Hands!  Piano tempo 50 and then spin it to 90.  

6) Tireless Butterfly
Inspired by busy ladies I know that work until they drop.

7) The Happy Waltz
Reminds me of my Ballroom Dance classes at Butte College and Chico State University

8) Kitty 2
Time signature: 5/8.  My 2nd piece.  The 1st was Kitty.

9) Pleasantville
Just as in the movie of the same name, you have to pass through a musical curtain of sound to get to the simple melodies & out again

10) Appleseed
In the style of my piano hero Elton John.  A nice Piano and Flute piece.  Just felt like an Apple today.

11) Midnight Jam
I love Jazz, and distortion Guitar, and sweet Trumpet.  

12) Flight of the Hummingbird
Inspired by Flight of the Bumblebee.  

13) Persistently getting an “A”
It's amazing how many types of sounds you can play with a single note repeating in the left hand.

14) Sunrise Overture
I like my “Fargo” sound bank.  It's the one that gives that funny distortion in the background.

15) All Work No Play
Waltz.  A little minor, a little major.  Could be music to movie “A.I.”

16) 343 Distortion
Playing with my Sound bending wheel.  It was 3:43 pm.  

17) In the Spring of 1066
Back to the Norman Invasion of 1066.  Distortion again.

18) Ice Age Winter's Impression
Starts harsh, ends with a calming of the weather.

19) Trance
Based on a cool sounding chord progression.  Rhythmic and Fun.

20) Thankful and Brilliant
Jazz.  My wife says it reminds her of a Joe walking down the street in the morning to a coffee shop.

21) Pan Flute and Guitar Duet
These two instruments are awesome together.

22) Orange Julius
Syncopation, triplets, playing with the percussion.
