SOLUTION for this Problem, Tested and works fine on all devices.
 
It happened for me, I have 3 devices (Tablet + 2 phones) and some smart device like google home mini, it is 3 days that I get this error continuously, as all of you:
 
Issue:
When I try to login into my Gmail account then it says suspicious activity detected and it takes me to the account recovery option. when I finally change my password in recovery it works on that device. but when i try to login into my other device with the new password then it again asked me to secure my account which I already did and takes me to account recovery and force me to change my password again.
 
How to resolve:
You can do it from your PC/Mac Chrome.
  • Go to your Chrome settings (From menu or press Alt + e when your chrome is open, then select Settings from menu which is opened on the right top corner)
  • Then in the Search bar on the top of the screen search for: clear
  • Then select the first option which is: Clear browsing data
  • Go to the Advanced Tab
  • In the first dropdown which is time range, set it on: All time
  • Select all boxes below it which are related to your different data on your chrome (see Comment Section: try Cache first, then restart browser)
  • Now, at the button of this page click on the Sign out, make sure you do it otherwise it will delete all of your chrome data from all of your connected devices.
  • Then click on the blue button which is Clear Data
  • When it is done, click on the Sign in in the button of this page (the where you click on the sign out in the step 7)
  • Now all should be done and you are ok to back to your normal life.
