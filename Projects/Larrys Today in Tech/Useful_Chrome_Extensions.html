  •  Non-Stop to keep playing without stopping when asked “Are you still listening?”
  • dotEPUB   Convert any webpage into an e-book
  • Floating for YouTube  Always on top Floating MiniPlayer for YouTube
  • Full Page Screen Capture  Capture a screenshot of your current page in entirely and reliably - without requesting any extra permissions.
  • Google Docs Online  Edit, create, and view your documents, spreadsheets, and presentations - all without internet access.
  • Google Translate  View translations easily as you browse the web.
  • High Contrast  Change or invert the color scheme to make webpages easier to read
  • Honey  Automatically find and apply coupon codes when you shop online.
  • iMacros for Chrome  Automate your web browser.  Record and replay repetitious work.
  • PotPlayer YouTube Shortcut  Quickly play or add a YouTube video (playlist).  Switch to PotPlayer using buttons on a page, also via context menu and middle click.
  • Read Aloud  A Text to Speech Voice Reader.
  • RSS Feed Reader  RSS and Atom feeds in toolbar
  • Save to Google Drive  Save web content or screen capture directly to Google Drive
  • Tab Resize - Split Screen Layouts
  • Tab-Snap  Get open tab URLs for window, or load a list of URLs into new tabs on this window.
  • TinEye Reverse Image Search  Find higher resolution versions of an image.
  • Wikiwand  Wikipedia gets a great new look
