To match and display a line preceding a specific phrase in the Notepad++ "Find results" window, you can use a regular expression with a lookahead assertion. 
Here is how to do it:

I.  Steps to Find the Previous Line

  • Open the Find dialog box by pressing Ctrl + F or going to Search → Find....
  • Go to the Mark tab.
  • In the Find what: field, enter the following regular expression:

(?-s)^.*$\R(?=.*YOUR_SEARCH_TERM)


    • Replace YOUR_SEARCH_TERM with the actual phrase you are searching for.
    • Explanation of the Regex:
      • (?-s) ensures that the . (dot) character does not match newline characters.
      • ^.*$ matches the entire previous line from the beginning (^) to the end ($).
      • \R matches any kind of line-ending sequence (CR, LF, or CRLF).
      • (?=.*YOUR_SEARCH_TERM) is a positive lookahead assertion that checks if the line immediately following the current position contains YOUR_SEARCH_TERM, without including that line in the actual match.
  • Check the Regular expression option.
  • Check the Bookmark line option.
  • Click the Mark All button.
Extracting the Results
The previous lines will now be bookmarked (indicated by a blue circle in the margin). To "print" or view these in the Find results panel as requested:
  • Go to the Search menu.
  • Select Bookmark.
  • Choose Copy Bookmarked Lines.
  • Open a new, empty tab (Ctrl + N).
  • Paste the contents (Ctrl + V). This new document will contain only the lines that appeared immediately before your search term.
You can also use the Search → Bookmark → Remove Unmarked Lines option to remove all other lines from your current document, leaving only the bookmarked (previous) lines. 

II. Steps to Find the Line after the Next Line


To "print" (which means extract or copy to a new location) the second line after a successful match in Notepad++ using regular expressions, you can use the Mark feature and then copy the results. This method effectively filters the entire file to only show your desired lines.

Step-by-Step Guide

  • Open the Find dialog in Notepad++ by pressing Ctrl + F.
  • Go to the Mark tab.
  • Check the Bookmark line option.
  • Check the Regular expression option.
  • Ensure . matches newline is UNCHECKED.
  • Use the following regex in the Find what field: 

^YOUR_SEARCH_TERM.*?\R\K.*

Replace YOUR_SEARCH_TERM with the specific text on the first line 
you are looking for.

^ matches the beginning of a line..
*? matches any character (except newline) non-greedily until the next 
part of the pattern.
\R matches any kind of line ending (CR, LF, or CRLF).
\K tells the regex engine to discard everything matched so far 
from the final result, ensuring only the second line is marked
..* matches the entire content of the second line.

Click Mark All. All lines immediately following your search term 
(the "second lines") will be highlighted and bookmarked.

Copy the marked lines:Go to the menu bar: Search > Bookmark > Copy 
Marked Lines.

Paste into a new tab: Open a new document (Ctrl + N) 
and paste (Ctrl + V) the copied text. 

This new document will contain only the extracted second lines.
