When there is no Chapter or Timeline Given.

Problem: Change Format to: 00:00:00 Fact ### Topic Description

Solution: 6 Steps

  • Copy YouTube Transcript
  • Isolate DATA
  • Eliminate Spaces and Dashes
  • All on One Line
  • In This Example Start With the word “Fact“
  • Show \1 \3
  • Paste Timeline into Comment Section of YouTube
  • Click, Listen, Complete the Incomplete Titles

STEPS with Examples 

1. Isolate DATA
MARK:     (?-s)^.*$\R(?=.*Fact\s)^.*$\R
Copy / Paste

2. Eliminate Spaces and Dashes
REPLACE:  (\R)----(\R)
with:     BLANK

3. All on One Line
REPLACE:  (\d)\R
with      \1 
          (Slash and 1 and Space)
Copy / Paste (For Display Purposes ONLY)

4. Start With the word Fact

EXPERIMENTAL: ISOLATE PARTS: \1 \2 \3
PART 1: ^(\d{2}:\d{2}:\d{2})
PART 2: (.*)
PART 3: (Fact(.*)\R)
ALL-TOGETHER: ^(\d{2}:\d{2}:\d{2})(.*)(Fact(.*)\R)

5. Show \1 \3
REPLACE: 
^(\d{2}:\d{2}:\d{2})(.*)(Fact(.*)\R)
with
\1 \3

RESULT:  (Output 1)

   Click, Listen, Complete the Incomplete Titles
