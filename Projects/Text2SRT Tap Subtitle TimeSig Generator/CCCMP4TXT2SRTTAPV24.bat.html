CCC_MP4_TXT2SRT_TAP_V24.bat


Program:  Create Subtitle “File.srt”
Requires:
  • “File.mp4” Movie
  • “File.txt”  FLAT FILE Lyrics without Time Signatures
  • CCC_TXT2SRT_TAP_V24.bat
Procedure:
  • DRAG/DROP “File.mp4” on to CCC_TXT2SRT_TAP_V24.bat
  • After Program starts Playing “File.mp4”, Manually click back to CMD Window to Give Focus
  • FIRST TIME: Click ONCE at beginning of Singing or Talking
  • FOR-EACH New Set of 2 LINES: Click ONCE at the end of Singing or Talking.
  • At End of Song, Click ONCE to establish the LAST Termination Time
  • Click ONCE to END Program
  • OPEN “File.mp4 with ”Subtitle Edit” and make Synchronization adjustment.
  • DONE!
