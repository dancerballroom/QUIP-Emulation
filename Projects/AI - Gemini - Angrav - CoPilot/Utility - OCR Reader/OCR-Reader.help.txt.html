OCR (Optical Character Recognition) Video to Text (OCR)

1.  Drag/Drop Video
2.  Find Location of Video Text
    (Right Arrow/Left Arrow) -+ 1 Minute
	(Cntrl+Right Arrow/Cntrl+Left Arrow) -+ 10 Seconds
	(Cntrl+Shift+Right Arrow/Cntrl+Shift+Left Arrow) -+ 1 Seconds
3.  Highlight Text
    Mouse Down = Set Top Left
	Mouse Up = Set Bottom Right
	+/-  Increase/Decrease Size of Selection
	Mouse Down/Up to Reset OCR Selection
4.  Press "OCR" Button to Capture text in Editable Text Area
    Output:  HH:MM:SS Text

NOTE: Depending on the Size of your Box the OCR can take a long time.
The Timer is in the Left Corner with Last OCR and Average OCR.
Average OCR Decode Time Depends on many factors.
Here is approximately the time it takes for:

1 short Line = 4 Seconds to 1 Minute
4 short Lines = 1 to 3 minutes
A page full of text = 10 to 20+ Minutes 

NOTE: Show OCR debug crops is for debugging.
