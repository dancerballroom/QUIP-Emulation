HOME-C-BACKUP.bat

Puzzle-This:
  • Your Drives are Mirrored with the following command: 
robocopy C:\Source D:\Destination /E /PURGE /XO /XN /Z /r:5 /w:5
  • However you don’t want to backup all of C:\.  
  • You Just want to automate the Update Procedure for Certain Directories on External Hard Drive

HOME-C-BACKUP.bat

@Echo Off
CALL robocopy "C:\BATCH-SCRIPTS" "E:\BUMBLE-BEE\C-DRIVE\BATCH-SCRIPTS" /E /PURGE /XO /XN /Z /r:5 /w:5
CALL robocopy "C:\JELLYFIN_HOME" "E:\BUMBLE-BEE\C-DRIVE\JELLYFIN_HOME" /E /PURGE /XO /XN /Z /r:5 /w:5
CALL robocopy "C:\kirk" "E:\BUMBLE-BEE\C-DRIVE\kirk" /E /PURGE /XO /XN /Z /r:5 /w:5
CALL robocopy "C:\FFMPEG" "E:\BUMBLE-BEE\C-DRIVE\FFMPEG" /E /PURGE /XO /XN /Z /r:5 /w:5
CALL robocopy "C:\POWER-SHELL-SCRIPTS" "E:\BUMBLE-BEE\C-DRIVE\POWER-SHELL-SCRIPTS" /E /PURGE /XO /XN /Z /r:5 /w:5
CALL robocopy "C:\QUIP.com" "E:\BUMBLE-BEE\C-DRIVE\QUIP.com" /E /PURGE /XO /XN /Z /r:5 /w:5
CALL robocopy "C:\VBS Scripts" "E:\BUMBLE-BEE\C-DRIVE\VBS Scripts" /E /PURGE /XO /XN /Z /r:5 /w:5
CALL robocopy "C:\ZAP-Commercials" "E:\BUMBLE-BEE\C-DRIVE\ZAP-Commercials" /E /PURGE /XO /XN /Z /r:5 /w:5
