Exporting Multiple MP3 using ShotCut

AI - Inquiry Answer

Yes, you can export multiple clips in Shotcut by adding individual clips to the Playlist view, then selecting "Each Playlist Item" from the "From" menu in the export panel to export them as separate files. To do this, you'll first need to split your long video into smaller clips on the timeline, then select each clip on the timeline and add it to the Playlist using the '+' button. 

(SPLIT/SELECT/COPY/PLAYLIST ADD(+)/EXPORT(DROP-DOWN:Each Playlist Item)/PRESET(MP3))


Here are the detailed steps to export multiple clips from one video in Shotcut:

  • Split the video: Load your video into Shotcut and drag it to the timeline.
  • Create clips: Move the playhead to the desired start point for a clip and press the 'S' key to split it at the playhead. Repeat this for all the sections you want to export.
  • Copy clips to playlist: Select the first clip on the timeline, copy it (Ctrl+C), then open the Playlist pane. Click the '+' button in the playlist to add the clip.
  • Repeat for all clips: Repeat the previous step for every clip you want to export.
  • Open export: Go to the Export tab.
  • Select "Each Playlist Item": In the "From" dropdown menu, select the "Each Playlist Item" option.
  • Export: Choose your desired export preset and click the "Export file" button. You'll be prompted to name the file and choose a folder. Shotcut will then export each item from the playlist as a separate video file.
